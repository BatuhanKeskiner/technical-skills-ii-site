Look up

the Exposure Formulap.66-67
key stop = the square root of the ASA. At the key stop, the shutter that puts a surface on Zone V is one over its luminance in candles per square foot. ASA 125 gives f/11. The exposure triangle in one sentence.
reciprocity effectp.41-42, table p.45
E = I x T holds in the middle and fails at the ends. Metered 1 second wants 2; metered 10 wants 50; metered 100 wants 1200 - twenty minutes. Adams refuses the word failure.
the zones, and print valuesp.64-68
Zone V is the metered mid-grey and every zone is one stop from it. Value 0 is the maximum black the paper can give and Value X is the paper base; those two are the scale's key reference values.
half zones and thirdsp.68
A zone is a RANGE of greys and the named value is its midpoint - so rounding to a name is what a name means. Halves and thirds are available when precision is wanted.
filter and extension factorsp.41
They multiply. They do not add. The commonest error in this area.
metering technique, five rulesp.45
the Exposure Record formp.67
